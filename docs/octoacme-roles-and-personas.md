# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Technical Leads** on architecture and design decisions
- Work with **QA/Testing Leads** to ensure testability and acceptance criteria are met
- Partner with **DevOps Engineers** on deployment and CI/CD integration
- Consult with **Security Officers** on secure coding practices
- Receive requirements and feedback from **Product Managers**
- Support **UX/Design Leads** in implementing user-centered designs

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Define quality acceptance criteria with **QA/Testing Leads**
- Collaborate with **UX/Design Leads** on user research and usability validation
- Align with **Project Managers** on timeline and scope
- Report to **Sponsors/Stakeholders** on progress and business impact
- Work with **Developers** and **Technical Leads** on feasibility

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with **Scrum Masters** to facilitate delivery and remove blockers
- Coordinate with **Technical Leads** on architecture decisions and technical risks
- Align with **QA/Testing Leads** on quality gates and release readiness
- Manage dependencies with **DevOps Engineers** on deployment schedules
- Partner with **Product Managers** on roadmap alignment
- Report to **Sponsors/Stakeholders** on progress and risks

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality strategy, testing approach, and acceptance validation. They work with developers and product managers to define testability requirements and ensure features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria with Product Manager
- Create and maintain test plans and testing strategies
- Conduct acceptance testing and sign-off on feature completion
- Identify and triage quality issues and regressions
- Mentor team members on testing practices and tools
- Participate in sprint planning and release planning

### Goals
- Deliver defect-free, production-ready features
- Catch quality issues early to reduce release risk
- Establish consistent quality metrics and reporting

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Test result summaries and quality metrics dashboards
- Issue triage and root cause discussions
- Release readiness sign-off

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Developers** to ensure testability and code quality
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** to define acceptance criteria
- Work with **Technical Leads** on test architecture and strategy
- Coordinate with **DevOps Engineers** on automated testing in CI/CD pipelines
- Provide sign-off to **Project Managers** on release readiness
- Support **UX/Design Leads** on usability testing and user acceptance

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, conduct design reviews, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with platform standards. They mentor developers and help identify technical risks early.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and architecture decisions
- Identify scalability, performance, and security concerns
- Establish coding standards and best practices
- Mentor junior developers and conduct code reviews
- Identify and propose mitigations for technical risks
- Collaborate with DevOps and Security teams on infrastructure and compliance

### Goals
- Ensure technical excellence and long-term maintainability
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build scalable, secure systems

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and guidance
- Technical risk assessments
- Decisions on technology choices and trade-offs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Mentor and review work from **Developers**
- Work with **Security Officers** to ensure secure architecture
- Collaborate with **DevOps Engineers** on infrastructure scalability and deployment
- Partner with **Project Managers** to identify and manage technical risks
- Support **QA/Testing Leads** on test automation and quality architecture
- Advise **Product Managers** on technical feasibility

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet security requirements and regulatory standards. They conduct security reviews, manage compliance assessments, and establish secure coding practices.

### Responsibilities
- Review features for security vulnerabilities and compliance risks
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Conduct security assessments and penetration testing (or coordinate with external teams)
- Maintain security documentation and compliance records
- Escalate security incidents and coordinate incident response
- Provide security guidance and training to the team

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and compliance violations
- Build customer trust through secure, compliant products
- Reduce risk exposure

### Typical Communication
- Security review checklists and threat assessments
- Compliance documentation and audit records
- Incident escalations and security advisories
- Security training and awareness updates

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Conduct security reviews with **Developers** and **Technical Leads**
- Define security acceptance criteria with **Product Managers**
- Collaborate with **DevOps Engineers** on infrastructure security and monitoring
- Escalate security risks to **Project Managers** and **Sponsors/Stakeholders**
- Provide security requirements to **QA/Testing Leads**
- Coordinate incident response across the entire team

---

## DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers own deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and operational readiness. They ensure reliable, scalable deployment and production monitoring.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and CI/CD infrastructure
- Manage staging and production environments
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting
- Conduct capacity planning and infrastructure scaling
- Coordinate rollback procedures and incident response
- Document runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, safe deployments
- Minimize downtime and operational incidents
- Provide observability and debugging capabilities

### Typical Communication
- Deployment windows and release coordination
- Infrastructure and monitoring dashboards
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Runbooks and operational documentation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Support **Developers** in CI/CD pipeline integration and deployment
- Integrate **QA/Testing Leads** automated tests into deployment pipelines
- Collaborate with **Technical Leads** on infrastructure architecture and scalability
- Work with **Security Officers** on infrastructure security and compliance
- Provide release readiness coordination to **Project Managers**
- Support **Sponsors/Stakeholders** with production monitoring and SLAs

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads ensure that features are user-centered, intuitive, and meet usability standards. They collaborate with Product and Development teams throughout the design and delivery process.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Collaborate with developers on implementation and design consistency
- Validate solutions meet user needs and usability standards
- Establish design systems and UX best practices
- Iterate on user feedback and analytics

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-friendly solutions
- Maximize user adoption and satisfaction
- Maintain design consistency across the platform

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Usability testing results and recommendations
- Design specifications and component libraries

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Product Managers** on user research and requirements
- Collaborate with **Developers** to ensure designs are implemented correctly
- Work with **Technical Leads** on design feasibility and performance
- Coordinate with **QA/Testing Leads** on usability acceptance testing
- Support **Project Managers** with design milestone planning
- Provide design requirements to the entire team

---

## Scrum Master/Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Facilitators help teams execute efficiently by removing blockers, facilitating ceremonies, and maintaining process discipline. They support the Project Manager in keeping the team aligned and productive.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, and retrospective meetings
- Identify and help remove team blockers
- Coach team members on agile practices
- Maintain project board and sprint tracking
- Escalate process impediments to Project Manager
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement

### Goals
- Keep the team focused and productive
- Maintain predictable velocity and delivery cadence
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation and updates
- Retrospective notes and action item tracking
- Sprint velocity and burndown metrics
- Process improvement recommendations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Support all team members (**Developers**, **QA/Testing Leads**, **Technical Leads**, **DevOps Engineers**, **UX/Design Leads**) in removing blockers
- Work with **Project Managers** to escalate process impediments
- Facilitate communication between **Product Managers** and the delivery team
- Coach **Developers** on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Capture retrospective insights from all roles for team learning

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business interests, provide funding and prioritization guidance, and ensure projects align with organizational strategy. They may be executives, department heads, or key customers.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic objectives
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Participate in decision gates and major milestone reviews
- Communicate project status to their organization
- Escalate and resolve organizational blockers
- Approve scope changes and trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Align project outcomes with organizational strategy
- Remove organizational barriers to success

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and demos
- Decision gate approvals and milestone reviews
- Escalation and resolution of blocking issues
- Executive briefings and business outcome reporting

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Receive strategic guidance to **Project Managers** and **Product Managers**
- Approve resource allocation for **Developers**, **Technical Leads**, **QA/Testing Leads**, and other team members
- Review milestone progress from **Project Managers**
- Receive business outcome metrics from **Product Managers**
- Escalate organizational blockers affecting the entire team
- Provide executive visibility to project status and risks

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The **Interactions with Other Roles** sections clarify how each persona collaborates with others, reducing ambiguity and improving cross-functional alignment.
